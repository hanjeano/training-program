Go-to meals to pick from when you don't want to think about it. Whole foods, a protein anchor in each, no counting.

Breakfast

3 eggsGo-toScrambled, fried or boiled. The everyday default: nothing to prep, nothing to decide.
Eggs, veg and avoEggs with spinach, tomato or mushrooms, and avo on the side.
Eggs, yoghurt and berriesEggs with a small bowl of Greek yoghurt and berries.
Yoghurt and granola bowlGreek yoghurt, granola, berries and a drizzle of honey.
Overnight oatsMake aheadMix the night before, eat cold. A full meal, not a dessert.
  • 250 g Greek yoghurt
  • 50 g oats
  • 1 tbsp peanut butter
  • 10 g honey
  • 80 g blueberries

Lunch

Chicken wrapChicken, salad and whatever veg is around, in a wrap.
Big saladGreens and veg with chicken, tuna or boiled eggs, olive oil on top.
Office plateWhatever's served: meat, veg and a carb. Protein first.
Pilchards or salmon on toastTinned, with the bones. Quick, and a good calcium top-up.

Dinner

Chicken, sweet potato and vegRoasted or pan-fried, with greens.
Fish, potato and vegGrilled or baked fish, potatoes, a big side of veg.
Lean meat, rice and vegBeef, lamb or chicken with rice and whatever veg is cooking.
Whatever's cooking at homeMost nights. Fill up on the protein and veg, and enjoy it.
