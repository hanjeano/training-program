My Training Program · KB, rings and bodyweight

Workout Library

Functional workouts for Friday on The Protocol, and Engine workouts for Thursday or the weekend. Log a result and it shows on the card next time.

How to use it

Functional · Fridays

  • Full-body KB, ring and bodyweight work: muscle-ups, squats, presses, cleans, swings and snatches.
  • Lean towards workouts with a hinge and power tag. The rest of the week is lightest on those.
  • Before a big match or round on Saturday, pick a lighter one.
  • Deload week: lighter bells and fewer rounds.

Engine · Thursday or weekend

  • Swap one in for Thursday's HIIT, or add one on Saturday.
  • Keep Sunday easy. Legs come first on Monday.

Progression

  • Repeat favourites every 2–3 weeks and log rounds, reps or the bell you used.
  • Beat a result twice, then go up a bell.
