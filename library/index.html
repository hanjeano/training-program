How it works

Daily rhythm

  • Morning: a mobility flow plus a walk or easy run.
  • Main session (30 min): 5 min warm-up and skill, 20 min main block, 5 min accessory.
  • Optional evening: swim or Zone 2 on days without a main session.

Rotation

  • Strength A → Complex → Strength B → Engine, then repeat.
  • Rotate session types, not weekdays. Miss a day and you pick up where you left off.
  • Aim for 4 main sessions a week. Three still works.

Balance rule

  • Every session: a lower movement, a push, a pull and an accessory.
  • Alternate squat and hinge from one session to the next.
  • If a workout misses a slot, your next session covers it.
  • Every Strength A ends with the knee block, every Strength B with the hamstring block. Keep both short and stop before failure. No Nordics the day before sprints.

Progression

  • Repeat favourites every 2–3 weeks and log rounds or reps.
  • Beat a result twice, then go up a bell.
  • 4-week waves: weeks 1–3 push, week 4 lighter bells and fewer rounds.
  • Strong: heavier bells, full rests. Lean: lighter bells, keep moving, add steps and Zone 2.
