The Protocol · guidance, not rules

Food

← Training

Training comes first. Food is there to support it: three good meals, simple and unmeasured. Good enough is the goal.

Principles

  1. Whole foods, mostly.
  2. A protein anchor in every meal.
  3. Three full meals a day, two on Sundays. No snacking.
  4. Train fasted, first meal after. Not training? Push the first meal later.
  5. Breakfast is protein and fat. Carbs come at lunch and dinner, with a proper carb on training days.
  6. Extras like fruit, granola or yoghurt go with a meal, never on their own. After dinner counts as dinner.
  7. An occasional longer fast is optional. Skip it if you're hungry or run down. Eat enough to train well.
  8. No counting, no making up for anything. Tomorrow is just tomorrow.

Go-to meals

BreakfastEggs, Greek yoghurt and berries. Every day, no deciding.
Sunday breakfastEggs, toast and avo.
LunchA wrap, a salad, or whatever's on the office plate.
DinnerWhatever's cooking at home: usually lean meat or fish, rice or potato, and veggies.

Flex, no strings

  • Add avo to breakfast whenever you feel like it.
  • Granola on top when you want it. No trading it off against anything.
  • Swap the yoghurt for a blueberry smoothie with whey now and then.
  • Fruit like kiwi, or yoghurt and berries, after dinner.
  • Missed the yoghurt today? It doesn't matter. It evens out over the week.

Optional fast

Dinner to dinner on the Zone 2 day, or the sport day when Zone 2 lands on a Sunday. Drink water and take electrolytes, especially around the sauna. Break it with a good dinner. The training page marks these days and shows tomorrow's, so nothing catches you off guard.
