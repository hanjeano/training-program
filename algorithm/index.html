The Algorithm

Legs, push and pull on their own days, an engine day with handstands, and a KB and rings day from the Workout Library. Weekends are for running and sport. Sessions take about 60 minutes. Food principles →

Progression

A weeks are 1, 3, 5, 7 and B weeks are 2, 4, 6, 8. When every set hits the top of the rep range, add load next time: 2.5 kg upper, 5 kg lower. After week 8, start again at week 1 with your new weights.

Principles

  • Session shape: 8 min warm-up, skill or power block, main lift(s), secondary, two accessory supersets.
  • Push day: bench first, then OHP, then weighted dips. Handstand holds only on push day; HSPU work lives on Thursday.
  • Knees and hamstrings: Nordics every Monday, plus reverse Nordics or sissy squats. A Strength B on Friday adds a second dose of Nordics.
  • Rotation for sport: med-ball rotational throws and anti-rotation work every push day.
  • Hinge: RDL in A weeks or hyperextensions in B weeks, never both.
  • Accessories: 1–2 reps short of failure, controlled.
