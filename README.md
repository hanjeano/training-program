## 2 Pattern Protocol

| # | Lower | Upper |
|---|-------|-------|
| 1 | Front Squat | Weighted Pull-Up / Chin |
| 2 | Conventional Deadlift | Bench (odd laps) / Dip (even laps) |
| 3 | Bulgarian Split Squat | Chest-Supported Row |
| 4 | Single-Leg RDL | OHP |

Opens on today's session with a one-line food note, shows the full 8-week schedule with off-day cardio, and turns your 1RMs into working weights (saved in your browser only). Printable version: `2pp/2-Pattern-Protocol.pdf`.

Cycle 1 runs 12 Oct – 6 Dec 2026. To start a new cycle, change `START` near the top of the script in `2pp/index.html` and in `index.html`.

## FBC

Block A supersets in weeks 1, 3, 5, 7 and Block B in weeks 2, 4, 6, 8. Sets and rest progress from 3 × 75 s to 4 × 45 s. Pick a week and day to see the warm-up, skill primer, supersets and finisher; changes from the original program are tagged. No start date yet.
