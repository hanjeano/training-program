# The Protocol

Training is the **2 Pattern Protocol**: an 8-week strength, power and conditioning program. Four workouts rotate every other day, each pairing one lower and one upper movement pattern.

**Live site:** https://hanjeano.github.io/two-pattern-protocol/

| # | Lower | Upper |
|---|-------|-------|
| 1 | Front Squat | Weighted Pull-Up / Chin |
| 2 | Conventional Deadlift | Bench (odd laps) / Dip (even laps) |
| 3 | Bulgarian Split Squat | Chest-Supported Row |
| 4 | Single-Leg RDL | OHP |

The site opens on today's session with a one-line food note (go-to breakfast, Sunday, or optional fast), shows the full 8-week schedule with off-day cardio, and turns your 1RMs into working weights (saved in your browser only). A printable version is in `2-Pattern-Protocol.pdf`. Food principles live on a separate page, `food.html`: guidance, not rules.

Cycle 1 runs 12 Oct – 6 Dec 2026. To start a new cycle, change `START` near the top of the script in `index.html`.
